How to play

You lead one continent. Nobody fights anybody else: every nation fights the aliens, and the world wins or loses together. Your MVP score ranks how much you did.

Win by destroying the mothership with the world barrage and freeing every alien city. Lose if every continent falls.

Explore through the fog to find your lost cities, all held by aliens, and storm them: a freed city is yours again. Cities only produce once a road joins them to your capital.

Citizens are born from spare food and grow up before they work. Each works one improved tile (farm, mine...) or sits on the council. Bare tiles cannot be worked, so keep engineers building.

Beacons call alien recruits; seat them on councils for bonuses. Trade, give aid and ship troops to help other continents.

Click a unit, then a highlighted hex to move or a red hex to attack (hover first for the odds). Enter: next turn · Space: next unit · F: fortify · S: skip · T/C/D/P: screens · Esc: menu · drag or arrows: pan · wheel: zoom.

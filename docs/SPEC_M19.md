# SPEC M19: months, the galactic winter, and better farms

Designer 2026-11-18.

## Months
- A turn is one month (it was a quarter). The top bar shows the month and year (Mar 2025).
  The leader ages a year every 12 turns. Nothing else counts years.

## The galactic winter
- A random event (seeded, so a seed always plays the same): alien dust thickens the sky for
  12 turns, a year.
- From turn 24, each turn with no winter on or coming has a 3% chance to start one, and never
  within 36 turns of the last winter ending.
- Scientists see it coming 3 turns ahead: a warning, then the winter.
- During the winter, crops lose part of their food: a farm or hydroponic plot keeps 50% of its
  food (farm bonuses included), a pasture 75%. Indoor food (buildings) is untouched.
- The city panel's food line shows the loss; the top bar shows the turns left.
- The AI pauses births in a town while winter leaves it short of food.

## Better farms and livestock
- Pasture (livestock), available from the start: 12 food and 1 production on grassland, plains
  or hills (flat or hills). Hardier in winter than crops.
- The tech tree's shape is fixed (2, 4, 8, 16), so the upgrades go on existing food techs:
  - Urban Planning (tier 1): farms +5 food (soil science).
  - International Accords (tier 2): pastures +4 food (shared breeding stock).
  - Vertical Farming (tier 3): cold-hardy crops - crops keep 25% more in winter (75%).
  - Climate Engineering (tier 4): +50% - crops and pastures lose nothing to the winter.
